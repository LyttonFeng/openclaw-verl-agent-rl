Mechanism (why)

  • In-dist tie: the generic how-to ("identify owners+deadlines", "map stakeholder influence", "group by topic") is method the base already largely does on these tasks → no lift.
  • Held-out harm: those advisory/gov/tech hints are mismatched/distracting on council votes/upcoming/contacts; injected into a 4B model they degrade output.
  • Latency cost: the mem's prompt overhead makes rollouts overrun the time budget → timeouts (held-out upcoming 4/4, in-dist gov 3/4) even at a 6× (1080s) budget.

Where this fits the project story

Third independent confirmation of the core lesson: signal derived in-distribution does not transfer — shown earlier for RL (H-E synthetic → no Val3 transfer) and mem-from-synthetic; now also for mem-from-real-training-data → held-out. And here, even in-distribution, generic-how-to mem adds no reliable lift over base.
